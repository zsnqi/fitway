# FITWAY — VDG-A review scorecard

## Review purpose

Use this scorecard to compare the three Google Stitch direction sets using the same facts and required artboards. The baseline application and screenshots are behavioral/information evidence only; resemblance to their composition earns no credit.

Directions under review:

- **PA — Premium Athletic**
- **OP — Operational Performance**
- **DK — Distinctive Kinetic**

Review order:

1. Complete the P1 direction preflight.
2. Verify package completeness.
3. Apply hard compliance gates.
4. Score the 17 comparison criteria.
5. Record direction-level strengths, risks, and rejection reasons.
6. Make an explicit decision and stop at the correct boundary.

An attractive direction that fails a hard gate is not eligible for approval until corrected.

## D. Scoring scale

## I. Review procedure

1. Confirm the completed P1 preflight record; this checkpoint is not a substitute for the full review.
2. Review all P1 artboards at the same size for first-glance clarity and pairwise difference in the completed package.
3. Review P2 immediately after each P1 to verify that closed state is a true composition, not a disabled live view.
4. Review P3 at actual 390×844 scale and inspect long Arabic, touch targets, and overflow.
5. Review P4 beside P1 for genuine RTL/LTR recomposition and fact parity.
6. Review S1 for visible preview status, live truth, health, control separation, and absence of fabricated success.
7. Review A1 by checking the plotted values against the supplied table, then compare its visible table row-for-row.
8. Review C1 for lifecycle, crowd, interaction, content-stress, focus, chart/table, and reduced-motion evidence.
9. Complete hard gates before scores. Correct fact/state/accessibility failures in Stitch within the same direction.
10. Score all eligible directions, record evidence, and choose: approve one, request a bounded revision, or reject all three.
11. Stop. Do not implement production UI or begin VDG-B/Phase 4 from this scorecard.

## J. Stop and continuation conditions

### Stop and return to Stitch when

- an artboard is missing;
- the P1 direction preflight is incomplete or a P1 pair is not genuinely distinct;
- shared facts or analytics rows drift;
- a hard compliance gate fails;
- two directions are palette/layout variants of the same composition;
- staff/analytics appear implemented or imply backend success;
- mobile, RTL/LTR, focus, reduced motion, or state honesty is not reviewable.

Repair the affected direction only. Keep its thesis. If the P1 preflight fails, repair only that P1 and do not expand it until the preflight passes. Do not create a fourth competing direction.

### Stop and request Hussein's decision when

- three complete, hard-gate-eligible, genuinely different direction sets are visible;
- all required views can be compared at their target sizes;
- scores, strengths, risks, and rejection reasons are recorded.

### After Hussein approves one direction

- Record the exact approval phrase, selected direction, rejected directions, rejection reasons, and anti-patterns.
- Claude Design may then consolidate/refine **only the approved direction** if Hussein chooses that workflow. It must not independently redesign Fitway, reopen the three-way exploration, or create a fourth direction.
- The approved visual contract, `FITWAY_DESIGN.md`, final `design-references/`, VDG-B implementation, and Phase 4 remain separate later work. None begins automatically from approval.

### Historical VDG-A failure conditions (retained for audit)

- only one or two directions exist;
- a direction lacks any required surface;
- the decision is based on a palette or isolated component;
- unresolved hard-gate failures remain;
- Hussein has not used the explicit approval language.
