# Access: the DESIGN-SPEC §4.4 fragment, and its new components for §3

For the coordinator to merge into `DESIGN-SPEC.md` (concept only, ADR-009): §4.4 below; each new component row into the
§3 or §2 section it names; the source `Ac1` into §0's source table; the amendments into the move to production.
Concept: `access.html`, `access.css`, `access.js`; frames from `access-capture.mjs`. No other page is edited; the rail's
and the bar's Access item on the other pages is still inert until the coordinator wires it.

**Source `Ac1`.** `access-capture.mjs` in this folder, run into `D:/fitway-temp/owner-r04-access/` (2026-10-04):
`access-log.json` and `crops/`: Access at 1440 × 900, 1024 × 768, 768 × 1024, 720 × 450 (the 200% zoom), 390 × 844 and
320 × 568, AR and EN, reduced motion, over HTTP on 3177 and from `file://`: the page at rest, its states, every action's
confirmation, working, done, failure and each refusal, the range cases, and the phone's flows by touch. Each frame is
checked for console errors, a font fetched twice, requests off the origin, layout shift at rest (and through the
loading's arrival), sideways scroll, elements past the viewport, spilling boxes and targets under 44 px; the one-time
view is checked to survive Escape (twice) and a click or tap outside; and after each view or change the PIN and the
passwords are looked for in the DOM, the page's hooks, the URL and every field.
