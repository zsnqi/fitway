# Owner r03 — early concept comparison

Open `index.html` through the local repository preview to compare four interactive Owner concepts with captures of the running synthetic demo. The comparison controls switch between Daily and a dense governance surface, English and Arabic, and exact `1440 × 900` and `390 × 844` frames. The current baseline selector offers Activity Log and Reports. Select a frame to inspect it at full size; each concept card opens its live interaction at the matching language and surface.

The four directions are deliberately different:

| Direction | Composition and interaction thesis | Governance slice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Temporal observatory | A broad day horizon, compact section rail, and direct time selection | Record-first Activity Log |
| 02 · Operations journal | Editorial state masthead, chapter-like rhythm, and a scrollable hour selector on mobile | Activity Log with record detail |
| 03 · Operational instrument | Layered controls, segmented day signals, and matrix inspection | Reports matrix |
| 04 · Evidence desk | Asymmetric signal wall, rectangular bars, and focused disclosure | Activity Log with record disclosure |

All captures use synthetic information. They are diagnostic and exploratory, not visual acceptance or production authority. Owner composition authority remains vacant. The user must choose, request a revision, or reject the directions before work expands to the full Owner surface set. A choice alone does not promote a concept to the production visual baseline.

From the repository root, serve the files with a loopback static server, then open `http://127.0.0.1:3115/design-research/owner-composition-exploration-r03/concepts/comparison/index.html`. Run `node design-research/owner-composition-exploration-r03/concepts/comparison/verify.mjs` for the repeatable image, link, locale, and reflow check. Each direction contains its own `README.md`, interaction checks, and captured frames under `evidence/`.
