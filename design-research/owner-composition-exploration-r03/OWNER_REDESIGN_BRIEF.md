# Owner workspace redesign — scoped concept brief

> Historical r03 input only. The user rejected every r03 concept on 2026-09-23 and stopped this design session. This brief is not an instruction for a future design agent, an accepted visual direction, a fixed direction count, or a staffing requirement. A new exploration starts from the user's latest feedback and a fresh scoped task.

## Human direction and scope

The user rejected all four r02 concepts: their colors, shapes, ordering, and chart treatments felt weak and too similar. They then clarified that the **running production Owner interface has a generally good visual character**—especially its color atmosphere and glass treatment—and asked for a substantial redesign within approximately that character, across the **entire Owner workspace**. The current page order, panel shapes, individual element/icon colors, icons, and weak or nearly absent motion are problems to solve, not constraints to preserve. The next directions must make consequential choices in layout, visual hierarchy, component forms, icon language, color roles, chart treatment, and animation. A safe polish pass or minor touches would miss the request. This is a positive visual reference in spirit, not approval of the incumbent composition and not permission to copy r02. No direction has been accepted or promoted.

The scope is shared Owner navigation and all six destinations: Daily, Reports, Access, Activity Log, Operations, and Settings. Preserve FITWAY identity, dark-only v1, Arabic-first RTL and English LTR, Cairo and Western digits, accessibility, privacy/security, and Product/Spec data semantics. Concept artifacts make no production or authority changes.

## Current rendered evidence

The coordinator inspected authenticated synthetic-demo frames from the running app under ignored `.local/demo/owner-inspection/`: Daily, Reports, Access, Activity Log, Operations, and Settings, each in EN/AR at 1440×900 desktop and 390×844 mobile; also Daily, Reports, Activity Log, and Settings full-page mobile. The frames were captured on 2026-09-23 through the documented local demo and are diagnostic evidence only. The repo inspector mapped the section routes to `OwnerSectionSwitch` and the section/view components. No live setting, credential, or account was changed.

Observed across surfaces:

- Shared navigation: the six-section strip clips its ends on mobile, without a clear continuation cue. Its first-screen space and position vary with each page header.
- Daily: the current near-black/oxblood atmosphere, translucent top rail, rounded integrated analytics board, and red curve are useful style anchors. On mobile, three stacked metric blocks delay the chart. The chart and coverage need a clearer first-read interpretation without implying false live data.
- Reports: two tall range/export cards precede the heatmap on mobile. The heatmap's horizontal hours are cut to the visible subset, and its interpretation follows below. The desktop view gives the date controls much of the opening viewport.
- Access: the default screen is readable and comparatively compact, but the same full-width high-emphasis red treatment serves routine access actions. A redesign should clarify hierarchy and risk without changing one-time reveal, account, or PIN behavior.
- Activity Log: on mobile, the filter form consumes the opening viewport and more before any record appears. The horizontally scrolled table initially shows time, actor, and target while the action and change are off-screen. The observed demo records cannot be read as a complete event at first glance.
- Operations: the default state is compact and the mobile event details are legible. The three top indicators have similar weight even though uptime, coverage, and alert delivery answer different questions. Keep their meanings distinct; do not infer an outage from a missing reading or collapse coverage into uptime.
- Settings: the current fields are readable, but the seven-day schedule becomes a long repeated mobile form before technical settings. Saved state and save control are remote from the lower edits. Keep the actual save/discard and time semantics intact.
- Icon and motion coherence: the user reports mismatched icon styles and icon colors across the current UI, and motion that feels almost absent. The screenshot audit cannot prove interaction timing; the next design session must inspect the running controls and transitions directly, then show a coherent icon/color system and visible, purposeful feedback in interactive concepts. Respect reduced-motion behavior.

The previous four concepts converged because their common brief specified nearly the same dark/red world and current-state-to-day-history flow, while the review gate occurred after full production. There is no evidence of corrupt design files. `pnpm check:design-context` passed when the sandbox allowed the installed engine to start. The previous r02 designs and their palettes, geometry, charts, and information order are rejection evidence, not visual references.

## Completed r03 comparison procedure (superseded)

The completed r03 comparison used four distinct **cross-surface concept slices**. Each slice rendered shared navigation, a Daily first screen with a chart and coverage explanation, and a dense governance surface (Activity Log or Reports), in EN/AR at 1440×900 and 390×844. The slices also demonstrated navigation, chart or detail interaction, control feedback, and reduced-motion handling. These were early alternatives for a human taste check; the user rejected all four. This procedure does not prescribe the number, staffing, palette, or composition of future alternatives.

All four stay recognizably within the production Owner character—its dark FITWAY atmosphere, color family, and glass quality—without locking the present placement, shapes, every element color, or one chart recipe. Supporting and semantic colors may change where that improves coherence and meaning. Each direction should design an intentional icon family, a clear color role for each icon/action/state, and motion that helps orientation and feedback rather than decorative noise. The directions may materially reinterpret material depth, panel geometry, navigation, rhythm, information order, chart grammar, form grouping, and responsive behavior. They may reorder the current content substantially while preserving factual and product semantics. Each must make its own observable spatial and interaction thesis; the four should not share one page skeleton or chart treatment. Do not simply rearrange the incumbent or import its CSS/tokens wholesale into the concept.

Before a full seven-surface implementation, place exact rendered frames side by side with the running current UI and ask the user to judge style continuity and usefulness. Inspect the interactive motion directly as well as the static frames. Evaluate visible differences in first-glance hierarchy, section finding, mobile density, chart reading, governance records, icon consistency, color roles, and transition quality. The user has set no generic cap on future idea count; four is the count for this bounded comparison because the original task requested four.

After a human chooses or revises a direction, the selected design must cover all seven surfaces and key states, EN/AR desktop/mobile plus 320px/200% reflow and tablet shell; preserve keyboard, focus, semantics, state truth, and every locked product contract. Concept choice and production/canonical promotion are separate explicit decisions.
