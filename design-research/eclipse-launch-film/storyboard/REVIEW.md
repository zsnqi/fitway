## Open after three rounds (said plainly)
- **The device thins out in the middle.** Through Reports the line is carried by the fill front, and in the montage by the small, still mark in the rail. Both are owned objects, but neither is the line itself. Station 2 should test whether the line can visibly hand over to the pattern's first row.
- **The hook is a premise, not a pain.** "Every day has a shape." opens inside the product, not inside the owner's problem (cinetic A2). The pain lens was rejected for truth (§2 of the treatment); this is deliberate, and the call is the user's.
- **The construction shows several red strokes at once** in its densest sixteenths (four in still 11). At motion speed only one or two read as red; Station 2 should measure accent coverage on the frames.
- **The laptop is procedural** and plain (no keys, no logo), dark graphite on a dark desk; its base reads a little flat. It is enough to carry the real screen; a finer body is a Station 2 choice.
- **The mark at 16 px** loses the gap between the dot and the inner arc. That is the supplied mark, not the film; Eclipse draws it at 26 px.
- **Stills 05 and 08 are wide UI frames with no film copy**, so their small UI text is not readable at thumbnail size; in the film they hold 1.9 s each and are followed by readable frames.
