# Rules in force

Every user note becomes a rule here. Rules are only added, never quietly dropped. Sources: user, refs A/B/C, Emil, the
Motion Lab article, cinetic.

## Picture
- Use the real Eclipse screens from `vendor/eclipse/` only. Never redraw a screen when the real one can be shown, and
  never invent a colour: take every colour from Eclipse's tokens (near-black ink, chalk, FITWAY red, the live green).
- One accent, red. Eclipse's red glow and gradients are brand-supplied exceptions to cinetic's glow and gradient bans;
  mark them `// cinetic:brand-supplied`.
- One hero move per shot. Secondary moves travel 50–70% as far and start 100–200 ms later.
- Typeface: Readex Pro (Eclipse's own, self-hosted), at real weights only. Use no serif and no italics.
- Banned: purple or blue, particles, lens flares, bloom halos, glassmorphism, confetti, emoji, "Introducing…",
  eyebrow labels, stacked taglines, fade-and-slide-up on everything, text moving while it is read, and fake metrics.
- Use only transform and opacity for motion. Use seeded randomness only. Every frame is a function of t.

## Copy
- Copy arrives in fragments of 3–7 words. A card caption is a 2-word label plus one line of 10 words or fewer.
- Hold each line at least 0.3 s per word + 0.5 s (about 1.0–1.2 s for a short line); holds drift and never freeze.
- The count is always approximate ("approx."). Make no claims beyond what Eclipse shows.

## Pace
- Median shot about 3–4 s, with 2–3 fast bursts of 0.3–0.6 s shots (the montage).
- One flat 2.5–3 s hold before the mark. The lockup holds 3.5–5 s.

## Sound
- Movement is silent. Only landings, taps and keys are heard, and there are no whooshes.
- A visual hit comes 1 frame before its sound.
- The music's biggest lift lands on the device reveal. The music resolves, not peaks, as the mark locks up.
- Audio starts at about 0.2 s and fades over the last 0.8 s.
