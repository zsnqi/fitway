# FITWAY — VDG-A direction matrix

## How to use this matrix

This matrix defines three visual hypotheses for Google Stitch to explore. It is not a set of finished designs, palettes, or implementation instructions. The shared product facts and hard constraints in `FITWAY_STITCH_BRIEF.md` and `FITWAY_CONTENT_FIXTURES.md` override all direction language.

Each direction must solve the same seven-artboard set. A direction remains internally coherent across public, staff, analytics, English, mobile, and states views, but it must be structurally distinguishable from the other two before colors or labels are inspected.

## One-sentence theses

- **Premium Athletic:** Fitway as a refined arena instrument—spacious, high-confidence, and brand-forward, with the occupancy truth staged like a premium scoreboard rather than enclosed in a generic card.
- **Operational Performance:** Fitway as a disciplined control surface—dense, explicit, and system-status-forward, with structured rails, strong alignment, and chart/table parity.
- **Distinctive Kinetic:** Fitway as a directional movement system—rhythmic, asymmetric, and energetic, using track/lane composition to connect count, band, and freshness without sacrificing operational honesty.

## Surface-by-surface direction requirements

### Premium Athletic

#### P1 / P4 — public live desktop

- Use an asymmetric, wide scoreboard composition rather than a centered portrait card.
- Occupancy band and `37` form the main visual statement; `37%`, open state, and freshness are visually attached to it, not scattered into equal cards.
- Concentrate Fitway red around brand/action emphasis; Moderate remains semantically distinguishable with its own label/icon and does not become red for brand consistency.
- The English artboard must rebalance the field for LTR.

#### P2 — public closed desktop

- Remove the occupancy instrument entirely.
- Let the closed status and `يفتح 2:00 م` occupy the premium focal stage without leaving a ghost meter or disabled count.
- The result should feel intentionally quiet, not empty or broken.

#### P3 — public live mobile

- Treat 390×844 as a portrait scoreboard, not a crop of desktop.
- Keep language control reachable, count dominant, full freshness readable, and all essential content within the initial viewport.

#### S1 — staff preview

- Use a refined control-console composition with live truth dominant and operational controls visibly separate.
- Avoid turning each fact into a luxury card. A few strong planes and a dedicated operations zone are preferred.

#### A1 — analytics preview

- Give the real chart a wide primary stage and the table an intentional ledger treatment.
- Peak value `84` at `8:00 م` should be findable without a tooltip; table/chart correspondence remains obvious.

#### C1 — states sheet

- Show premium material and typographic hierarchy across states, but do not hide operational differences behind subtle tint changes.

### Operational Performance

#### P1 / P4 — public live desktop

- Use a compact public instrument with a persistent status/freshness rail; do not use an isolated floating card.
- The first glance must still answer open/live and Moderate/37 quickly despite higher density.
- Labels and alignment should carry credibility more than large empty space.

#### P2 — public closed desktop

- Promote schedule state into the status rail and replace—not disable—the occupancy area.
- No operational diagnostics may leak onto the public page.

#### P3 — public live mobile

- Stack status and freshness before or immediately with the occupancy instrument; avoid desktop side rails compressed into a phone.
- Keep touch targets and Arabic lines comfortable even at the direction's higher density.

#### S1 — staff preview

- This is the direction's strongest surface: persistent device/freshness health, aligned count/capacity, and a compact but unmistakably separate operations panel.
- Do not show a successful command. Keep preview and “no command sent” labels visible.

#### A1 — analytics preview

- Chart and table have equal analytical authority.
- Use explicit axes, tick labels, capacity reference, row/column alignment, and data-quality labels. No unlabeled mini charts.

#### C1 — states sheet

- Favor compact, repeatable component states with explicit default/focus/disabled/pending differences.

### Distinctive Kinetic

#### P1 / P4 — public live desktop

- Build a legible directional composition using track/lane/band geometry. The geometry must encode reading order or occupancy progression, not merely decorate the background.
- Anchor band and `37` into one unmistakable landmark; place freshness on the same visual path so it clearly qualifies the count.
- No diagonal or curved treatment may distort text, reduce contrast, or imply that the count is continuously streaming.

#### P2 — public closed desktop

- End or pause the visual path at the closed state and next opening. Remove all occupancy progression.
- Preserve literal status text/icon; a paused line alone is not enough.

#### P3 — public live mobile

- Convert the path into a vertical reading sequence. No horizontal canvas, clipped off-screen track, carousel, or sideways scrolling.
- The count remains the first dominant data landmark.

#### S1 — staff preview

- Use one readable action lane for correction/direct entry/reset while retaining conventional inputs, buttons, and focus order.
- Keep health and freshness stationary and always visible; operational truth must not move through a carousel or ticker.

#### A1 — analytics preview

- A real line chart may echo the lane language, but its coordinates, values, peak, axes, and table mapping stay conventional and exact.
- No decorative waveform, speed lines, or implied prediction beyond the supplied points.

#### C1 — states sheet

- Prove that the visual language works when animation is off. Position, hatching, labels, and icons must preserve the concept in reduced motion.

## Pairwise difference tests

Before submitting a direction set, compare it with the other two:

1. **Silhouette test:** blur or thumbnail the three P1 artboards. Their primary shapes and negative-space patterns must be visibly different.
2. **Monochrome test:** ignore hue. Hierarchy and state meaning must remain different and usable.
3. **Navigation test:** authenticated concepts must not share the same navigation shell with minor styling changes.
4. **Hero test:** the relationship among count, band, percent, and freshness must be structurally different.
5. **Chart/table test:** each direction must have its own analytical grammar while plotting exactly the same values.
6. **Mobile test:** P3 must demonstrate three different portrait structures, not three desktop stacks.
7. **Motion-off test:** each direction remains identifiable and complete under reduced motion.

If two directions fail two or more tests, revise the weaker direction inside its existing thesis. Do not solve the problem with a new palette and do not create a fourth direction.
