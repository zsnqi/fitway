## Decisions and coordinator-ratifiable defaults

- Human-reviewed Phase 10 semantics govern business-day/timezone attribution, comparison windows,
  a default 0.8 comparison coverage constant with an optional input override, CSV safety/shape, the
  inclusive 366-day CSV cap, and the no-index/no-migration boundary. This rules out
  calendar-midnight/host-timezone aggregation, fabricated history, device fields, exposure wiring,
  or schema work in this slice.
- `sampleDayCount` is the distinct number of business days with at least one observed value minute
  in that weekday/local-hour cell. Closed and all-missing days do not count.
- Week metrics use the weighted observed-minute occupancy mean and estimated entrance crossings.
  Changes include absolute and fractional percentage values; a genuine zero prior denominator
  produces `percent: null`, never a fabricated ratio.
- CSV allowlist/order is: `business_day`, `minute_start_utc`, `minute_start_local`, `time_zone`,
  `state`, `count`, `entries`, `exits`, `band`, `capacity_snapshot`, `settings_version`, `source`.
  Closed/missing rows keep time/context/state and leave all numeric/snapshot/source cells empty.
- `CSV_MAX_RANGE_DAYS = 366` is a conservative resource guard for this slice, not a permanent
  product decision. Reconsidering it requires measured evidence and coordinator/product authority.
- CSV uses a fixed-name cursor on a dedicated pooled connection inside a repeatable-read, read-only
  transaction. One bounded `SELECT` is fetched in batches (default 1,000), grouped by business day,
  classified with the effective settings timeline, and encoded incrementally.
- The representative performance fixture is 90 consecutive business days / 129,600 one-minute
  rows. Current authority defines no pass threshold; the measurements below are evidence, not a
  schema-change decision.
