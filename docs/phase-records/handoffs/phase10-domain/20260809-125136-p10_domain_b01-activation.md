## Contracts and invariants this slice must honour

From `SPEC.md` "Analytics semantics" and Phase 9's frozen shape:

- Business-day and gym-timezone aware throughout. Never calendar midnight, never host timezone.
- `closed`, `missing`, and genuine zero stay three distinct states and never collapse. `closed` and
  `missing` carry a null count.
- Classification uses the settings version effective at each instant, resolved from the append-only
  `settings_versions` timeline by `effectiveFrom` — not from today's settings. A gap has no row.
- Band and capacity come from the row snapshots (`band`, `capacity_snapshot`), never from current
  settings. Historical results stay immutable after later settings changes.
- Averages include genuine zero rows and exclude closed and missing minutes; report observed and
  expected open minutes so outage coverage is never hidden.
- Week-over-week returns an honest empty state until two comparable weeks exist. It does not
  fabricate, interpolate, or zero-fill.
- "Daily visits" is the sum of entries per business day and is always the "estimated entrance
  crossings, not unique members" quantity.
- CSV: UTF-8, spreadsheet-safe, Western digits, both UTC and gym-local time columns, and only the
  authorized private analytics fields. Owner-only in effect — enforced at exposure, but the row model
  must not carry anything an owner may not see.
- Every module boundary declares Zod `.input()`/`.output()` schemas per `AGENTS.md` source-of-truth
  item 3, even though nothing is routed yet.

## Database and index assessment — no migration is authorized

Verified evidence at this commit:

- `occupancy_minutes` has primary key `(device_id, minute_start_utc)`
  (`packages/db/src/schema/application.ts`, `packages/db/src/migrations/0000_clean_riptide.sql`).
- No index on `business_day` exists anywhere; `grep "CREATE INDEX"` across
  `packages/db/src/migrations/*.sql` returns only auth, session, audit-log, and edge-command indexes.
- `apps/server/src/analytics-repository.ts` already filters `where business_day = ?` with no
  supporting index, and Phase 10 adds range predicates over the same column.

That makes an index *plausible* and nothing more. `docs/phase-records/dependency-parallelization-audit.md`
§16.2 and `docs/phase-records/wave-0-baseline.md` §"Unresolved, carried forward" item 5 both record
this as **UNVERIFIED, needing measurement**. `AGENTS.md` and `docs/WORKFLOW.md` put migration
generation in a single coordinator-serialized lane, and `packages/db/**` is in this slice's
`forbiddenPaths`.

Therefore: **the worker authors no migration and no index.** It instead produces measurement
evidence — `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` for the heatmap, comparison, and CSV range queries against a
representative row volume in its own disposable database, recorded verbatim in its closing handoff.
The coordinator decides afterwards whether a `0006` migration is justified and, if so, authors it.
Discovering a slow query is a handoff finding, not a licence to add an index.
