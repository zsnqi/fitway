## Decisions made

- `PriorAlertLog` suppression, re-alert eligibility, recovery linkage, and health-transition lookup are device-scoped, as required by the Phase 8 retry contract and `SPEC.md` 577–584.
- The repository takes a transaction-scoped Postgres advisory lock, re-reads the evaluation input, appends the decision claim, then calls the injected notifier and appends its immutable `delivered` or `failed` outcome. `claimed` is an explicit `0004` enum value; no existing log row is updated or deleted.
- Health flag transitions use the projection receipt of the qualifying change. Failure start derives from the first still-unresolved device transition, retains that time for re-alerts and recovery, and begins a new interval after recovery.
