## Goal and acceptance

Deliver the pure Phase 8 alert-evaluation domain per `SPEC.md` 577–584 (alert policy),
414–428 (current-health boundary), and `PHASES.md` Phase 8: deterministic condition
detection, suppression/re-alert policy, and append-only health-transition and alert-log
persistence. This slice is evaluator + storage only. Cron wiring, Telegram transport,
retention cleanup, and any UI belong to `phase8-integration`/`phase11-health` and are
forbidden here.

Evaluator/domain boundary (exact):

- Pure functions in `packages/api/src/alerts/**` over injected inputs only: clock,
  settings-driven thresholds (stale threshold, pre-open window default 30 min, re-alert
  interval default 30 min — config-driven, no hard-coded policy), the schedule module's
  open/closed/next-open answers (consumed read-only), the `edgeCurrentHealth` projection
  state, and prior alert/transition log state. No I/O, no ambient time, no randomness.
- Conditions per the frozen policy: (a) no accepted push ≥ the stale threshold during open
  hours or within the 30-minute pre-open window; (b) a push health snapshot reporting
  camera/feed/process failure; (c) at most one re-alert per condition every 30 minutes
  while unresolved; (d) failures arising during closed hours are suppressed but alert at
  pre-open if still unresolved; (e) a recovery notice when a condition clears. Alert and
  recovery decisions are distinct outputs; transport failure remains distinct from
  unavailable device health.
- Health transitions (offline/online and reported-flag changes) are derived as append-only
  events; the evaluator must not redefine, rewrite, or extend the `edgeCurrentHealth`
  current projection or the phase4 freshness/condition evaluator — both are consumed
  read-only.
- Delivery is behind a one-function notifier interface defined in this slice and faked in
  every test. No Telegram client, no HTTP, no `packages/env/**` change (bot token/chat id
  env additions are `phase8-integration` work).
- All evaluator decisions and delivery outcomes are recorded through the repository into
  the append-only logs; the log is the suppression/re-alert authority, so evaluation is
  replayable and idempotent for the same inputs.

Persistence (under the migration `0004` lease):

- Add append-only `edge_health_log` and `alert_log` tables per `SPEC.md` data model:
  health transitions with device reference and timestamps; alerts with type, condition
  started at, sent at, delivery outcome, and recovery linkage. No new writes to any
  existing table; no seed rows; retention cleanup is out of scope.
- `apps/server/src/alert-repository.ts` owns log reads/writes; the evaluator never touches
  the database directly.

Verification:

- Deterministic unit fixtures in `packages/api/src/alerts/**`: fixed clocks around
  open/close/pre-open boundaries (including past-midnight close and Friday hours),
  threshold equality edges, flapping failure/recovery, re-alert interval boundaries, and
  closed-hour suppression rolling into pre-open escalation.
- A disposable-Postgres integration test at
  `apps/server/src/phase8-alert-evaluator.integration.test.ts` proving: condition detection
  writes the alert log with a faked notifier; suppression/re-alert reads the log correctly;
  recovery linkage; health-transition append-only behavior; and that evaluation mutates
  nothing outside the two new tables (current state, occupancy minutes, and
  `edge_current_health` are byte-for-byte unchanged by evaluation).
- Public payload schema v2 remains untouched and free of capacity, percentage, health,
  device identity, and history.
