## Goal and acceptance

Replace the nonconforming staff email/password scaffold at the server/domain boundary with the
frozen Phase 4 PIN/principal/session/role model. This slice is backend-only and must not implement
`/login`, `/staff`, or `/admin` UI.

Acceptance requires tests proving:

- roles exactly `staff | owner` and principal kinds exactly `shared_staff | owner`;
- one shared staff principal with no synthetic email identity;
- a 6–12 Western-digit PIN, memory-hard salted hash, server-held pepper, and no stored/logged/
  seeded/URL raw PIN;
- opaque session tokens stored only as hashes;
- a signed session cookie with Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Path=/, no Domain,
  approximately 30-day rolling expiry, and refresh at most once per 24 hours;
- separately provisioned real owner accounts resolve to real owner principals/sessions, while
  direct signup remains disabled;
- expiration, revocation, deactivation, credential-version rotation invalidation, and direct
  signup rejection;
- canonical context fields `principalId`, `principalKind`, `role`, `sessionId`, `expiresAt`,
  `active`, including ambiguous valid owner/staff rejection and explicit-login clearing;
- staff-or-owner versus owner-only guards with exact `401`/`403` behavior;
- rate-limited, non-enumerating PIN failures; and
- real HTTP/disposable-Postgres integration with no raw secret in logs or fixtures.

Prefer built-in Node cryptography where it satisfies the contract. A new dependency or manifest/
lockfile change is outside the lease and requires coordinator approval.
